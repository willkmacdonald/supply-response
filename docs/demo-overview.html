The recommendation

A response the planner can inspect

The demo recommends the combined response for review. It is a prediction based on the saved evidence—not an approval, purchase commitment, or claim that the result has already happened.

Recommended for review

Combine the supplier's partial shipment, a Dallas transfer, and production resequencing

This response protects more customer demand than the other feasible choices while keeping the evaluated response cost within the scenario's Finance path.

  • Uses the 3,000-unit supplier recovery that has a confirmed date.
  • Adds 1,500 components available for transfer from Dallas.
  • Resequences production to protect the most valuable customer commitment.
  • Excludes Alternate Supplier Beta because qualification is still pending.
