# Vendor-Supported Path First

- Default to the vendor’s documented, supported workflow.
- Do not create custom authentication, orchestration, security, lifecycle, or infrastructure machinery unless:
  1. the standard workflow has been tested and shown not to meet a stated requirement;
  2. the specific deficiency is documented with evidence; and
  3. the user explicitly approves the custom alternative.
- “Proceed” authorizes the current agreed step only. It does not authorize expanding scope or raising the assurance level.
- Do not turn setup or integration work into a proof, certification exercise, or adversarial test program unless explicitly requested.
- Before materially expanding scope, stop and explain:
  - what the supported approach is;
  - why it is insufficient;
  - what additional work is proposed;
  - expected cost, complexity, and maintenance burden.
- Prefer a working conventional solution over proving every internal property.
- After repeated failure, reassess whether the approach is wrong before adding more controls, tests, or abstraction.
- Optimize for the user’s stated business objective, time, and token cost, not theoretical completeness.
